# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Companies or individuals looking for high-quality, conversion-focused landing pages. Traffic will come primarily from Google Ads.

## Product Purpose
To sell landing page creation services. The site will showcase the creator (Matheus) as a person (who he is) but pivot away from listing out technical skills or technologies used. It will serve as a portfolio using previously built landing pages as models/examples to persuade visitors to buy their own landing page.

## Positioning
A personal, human-centric approach to building landing pages, backed by a strong portfolio of already successful, high-converting models that clients can rely on.

## Operating Context
A visitor clicking a Google Ads link, arriving on this page, quickly reading about who Matheus is, seeing proof (models of other landing pages), and making a decision to contact or buy.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Must present the portfolio of previous sites effectively. Must avoid technical jargon about specific technologies, focusing on outcomes and human connection.
